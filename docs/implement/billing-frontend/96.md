## Ticket understanding

### Ticket (Trello #96, as retrieved 2026-10-11)

Part of #93, section 4. Depends on 2/8. Backend: Safepay in PR #27; bank transfer in PR #26.

1. **Payment step** — show the invoice before the redirect: number, plan, period, lines and total (Safepay's page shows only the amount, no merchant name or line items). Say the host will be taken to Safepay, HostelHive's payment provider. Offer only the methods in `billing.payment_methods` (PKR and USD both offer `safepay` and `bank_transfer`; Safepay charges in the invoice's currency).
2. **Pay with Safepay** — `POST /api/invoices/:id/payments {payment_method: "safepay"}` → 201 `{payment:{id, payment_method, amount, currency, status:{slug:"pending"}}, checkout_url, invoice}`. Redirect the browser to `checkout_url`; disable the button while in flight. Paying again reuses the session (safe to retry). Sandbox test card `4111 1111 1111 1111`, any future expiry/CVC; street address and city required on Safepay's form.
3. **Return pages** — Safepay sends the host back to:
   - `/billing/payment/success?invoice=:id`: "Confirming your payment…", poll `GET /api/invoices/:id` every 2–3 s for up to ~60 s until `invoice.status.slug == "paid"`. Paid → success, then refresh `/billing`. Timeout → "We're confirming your payment; you'll be notified." (webhook, or a ~25 min safety net; receipt email). The return page never decides success — only the invoice status does.
   - `/billing/payment/cancel?invoice=:id`: "Payment cancelled" + **Try again** (same invoice, same call).
4. **Bank transfer** — show the bank details for the currency and ask the host to quote the invoice number; optional proof upload via the existing presigned upload (`GET /api/<attachment_type>/presigned_url` → `{url, id}`, PUT the file, send `id` as `proof_attachment_id`); submit `POST /api/invoices/:id/payments {payment_method:"bank_transfer", reference, proof_attachment_id?}` → 201, payment `awaiting-verification`, `billing.open_invoice.awaiting_verification` becomes true. A second transfer while one is under review → 422 "A bank transfer for this invoice is already waiting for verification". An admin rejection leaves the payment `failed` with `failure_reason`; let the host submit again.
- **422s shown as returned:** "This invoice has expired" (→ back to the plan cards), "Invoice HH-… is paid", "Enter the bank transfer reference", "Online payment is unavailable right now, please try again (…)".
- **Acceptance:** sandbox card payment ends on the success page with the invoice `paid` and billing showing the active plan · cancelling on Safepay lands on the cancel page, Try again works · the success page times out gracefully after ~60 s · a bank transfer shows "under review" until an admin verifies or rejects · only `payment_methods` are offered.

#### 2. Backend contract (`azeem-hamza/hostelhive@main`)

##### a. `POST /api/invoices/:id/payments`

Sources: `app/controllers/api/invoices_controller.rb` and `app/services/billing/payments.rb`.

- **Params:** `payment_method` is required (`params.require`; a missing one gives 400). `reference` and `proof_attachment_id` are optional.
- **Success:** 201 `{payment, checkout_url, invoice}`. `checkout_url` is non-null only for Safepay, and `invoice` is the reloaded full invoice.
- **Authorisation:** `authorize!(:create_payment, @invoice)` for POST and `:show` for GET. The CanCan permissions are `core:Invoice:show` and `core:Invoice:create_payment`, granted to the host role by migration `20261004140000`. A 403 uses the singular-`error` envelope.
- **Locking:** the call runs inside `@invoice.with_lock`.

Validations run in this order. Each 422 is `{errors:[msg], success:false}`.
1. `check_payable!`:
   - "Invoice #{number} is #{status name downcased}" if the invoice is not open, for example "Invoice HH-2026-000012 is paid" or "…is void".
   - If the invoice is open but expired, it is voided and the error is "This invoice has expired". The void side effect is committed.
2. "#{Method humanized} isn't available for #{currency} invoices" when the method is not in `HOST_PAYMENT_METHODS`. Both PKR and USD allow `safepay` and `bank_transfer`.
3. Bank transfer:
   - "Enter the bank transfer reference" when `reference` is blank.
   - "A bank transfer for this invoice is already waiting for verification".
   - On success it creates a payment with status `awaiting-verification` and extends the invoice `expires_at` to at least now + 72h (`BANK_TRANSFER_TTL`).
4. Safepay:
   - "There is nothing to pay on invoice #{number}" when the total is zero.
   - "Online payment is unavailable right now, please try again (#{message})" on any Safepay error. The inner message is "Safepay request failed (code)" or "Couldn't reach Safepay: …".
5. An unknown `payment_method` value passes the first check only if it is in the allowed list. Any other value, such as "cash", gets the "isn't available" 422.
6. A missing or unknown invoice id gives 404.

**Payment serializer** (`app/serializers/payment_serializer.rb`)
- Fields are `id, status{id,name,slug,color}, payment_method, amount, currency, reference, gateway_reference, failure_reason, proof_url, verified_at, succeeded_at, created_at, invoice_id`.
- Status slugs come from the seeds: `pending`, `awaiting-verification`, `succeeded`, `failed`.
- The payment methods are `safepay`, `bank_transfer`, `cash` and `admin_grant`.
- `proof_url` is `Attachment#url`, which is `IMAGES_HOST/unprocessed/<namespace>/<type>/<id>`.
- `failure_reason` is free text. After a rejection it is the admin's reason. For a Safepay decline it is "Safepay declined the payment" or a webhook message. For a closed checkout it is "The Safepay checkout ended unpaid (TRACKER_…)". For a voided invoice it is "Invoice voided" or "Expired".
- A charged-but-unusable Safepay payment gets "…. Charged at Safepay (<tracker>); refund it from the Safepay dashboard" appended. That is internal wording that may show up in `failure_reason`.

**Second Safepay attempt**
- `Payments#safepay` first looks for an existing `pending` safepay payment on the invoice. If found it reuses the same `gateway_reference` (tracker) and does not create a second tracker.
- It always builds a **fresh `checkout_url`**, with a new passport token, for the existing tracker. Retry and double-click are safe, so Try again is just the same POST.
- If the earlier attempt was `failed`, for example Safepay declined it or the reconcile job found the tracker cancelled, a new tracker is created.
- Voiding the invoice fails all pending payments.

##### b. Safepay integration (PR #27)

- **Return URLs** are built in `Billing::Payments#return_url`:
  - It reads `ENV.fetch("SAFEPAY_SUCCESS_URL")` or `ENV.fetch("SAFEPAY_CANCEL_URL")`.
  - It appends `invoice=<invoice.obfuscated_id>` to whatever query the env URL already has.
  - Both URLs are full URLs held in environment variables. They are not hard-coded in the repo.
  - A grep of the whole tarball finds `SAFEPAY_SUCCESS_URL` and `SAFEPAY_CANCEL_URL` only in `payments.rb`.
  - So the frontend origin, the path and any locale prefix are deploy-time config. The only repo text on this is PR #27's body: "Safepay sends the host back to `SAFEPAY_SUCCESS_URL` / `SAFEPAY_CANCEL_URL` with `?invoice=:id`; the success page polls `GET /api/invoices/:id` until it is `paid` (frontend #93, section 4)."
  - The Trello text `/billing/payment/success?invoice=:id` is the card's contract. I cannot confirm what is set in the dev or production env.
  - **There is no locale handling.** Whatever the env holds is used. If it is `…/billing/payment/success`, that is the bare URL.
- **`checkout_url`:** `Safepay::Client#checkout_url` returns `https://sandbox.api.getsafepay.com/embedded/?` (or `https://getsafepay.com/embedded/?` in production). The query is `environment, tracker, tbt (passport token), source=hosted, redirect_url, cancel_url`. The `redirect_url` and `cancel_url` params carry the return URLs from the env.
- **Confirmation and safety net:**
  - **Webhook:** `POST /external/payments/safepay_webhook`, handled by `Billing::SafepayWebhook`. It then calls `SafepayPayments.confirm!`, which asks Safepay for the tracker, requires `TRACKER_ENDED` and the exact amount and currency, and then runs `BillingService.apply!`.
  - **Safety net:** `SafepayReconcileJob` runs every 10 minutes. It looks at pending safepay payments aged from 15 minutes to 3 days. A paid tracker is applied, and a cancelled or expired tracker is failed.
  - **Consequence for the card text:** the safety net triggers after 15 minutes of age and runs every 10 minutes, so a missed webhook settles in roughly 15–25 minutes. This agrees with the card's "~25 min" figure.
  - PR #27 notes that the sandbox produced a paid payment with no webhook at all, so the 60-second poll can time out on a successful payment in the sandbox.
- **`flag_for_refund`:** a card charged for the wrong amount, or for an invoice voided or paid meanwhile, is failed and flagged for a manual refund.

##### d. Presigned upload

Sources: `config/routes.rb`, `app/controllers/api/attachments_controller.rb` and `app/models/attachment.rb`.

- **Route:** `GET /api/:attachment_type/presigned_url`. The `attachment_type` is a **path segment**, not a query param.
- **Query params used:** `key` (a free-text grouping label), `file_name`, `content_type` and `label_id`.
- **Response:** `{success, url, id, object_url, message}`. `id` is the attachment's raw UUID (`skip_obfuscates_id!`), not an obfuscated id.
- **Valid types:** `Attachment.all_attachment_types = %w[documents generic avatars videos audios cnic_front cnic_back]`. Anything else fails model validation (`create!` raises, likely a 500 or 422).
- **Content type:** an unsupported `content_type` returns 422 "Content Type not supported". The map allows pdf, csv, txt, doc, docx, xls, xlsx, jpeg, jpg, avif, png, heic, heif, json, svg, xml, mp4, mov, m4v, mp3 and webm. Images and PDFs are both fine.
- **The FE already uses `documents` for everything.**
  - `ImageUploadService` and `DocumentsApi` both call `/api/documents/presigned_url` with `key` set to `avatar`, `logo`, `attachments`, `cnic_*`, `documents` or `receipts`.
  - So `documents` is the attachment type, and the receipts flow in `features/host/mess/add-grocery.ts:240` uses `presignedUrl(file.type, null, 'receipts')`.
  - A payment proof should use type `documents`, plus a `key` such as `payment_proof`.
- **Auth:** `presigned_url` has no extra `authenticate_host!`. `authenticate_user!` is not skipped on it.
- **`proof_attachment_id`:**
  - It must be the `id` UUID string returned by `presigned_url`.
  - It is stored as a string column (`rebuild_billing.rb:91`).
  - There is **no server-side validation** that it exists, has the right type or has finished uploading. `payment.proof` is `Attachment.find_by(id: …)` and `proof_url` is nil if the lookup fails.
  - It is not "attached" to anything, so `DeleteOrphanAttachmentsJob` might remove an unattached upload. The job was not read, and the risk is unverified.

##### e. `GET /api/invoices/:id`

- **Shape:** `{invoice:{…}}`. The invoice serializer fields are `id, number, kind, currency, total, period_start, period_end, expires_at, paid_at, voided_at, created_at, invoice_pdf_url, plan{id,code,name,features}, lines[{description,amount}], status{id,name,slug,color}` plus `payments[]` in the payment serializer shape above, ordered by `created_at, id`.

- **Authorisation:**

  - A user without the flag gets 403 `{success:false, error:"…"}`.
- **Gap against D7:** the nav permission is `core:Subscription:show`, but paying needs `core:Invoice:create_payment` and reading the invoice needs `core:Invoice:show`. A staff member with Subscription:show alone would get 403 on the pay page. The pay page needs its own inline 403 state.

#### Backend finding: the "expired" void is likely rolled back (hypothesised)

`check_payable!` calls `@invoice.void!("Expired")` and then `raise Error, "This invoice has expired"`, both inside `with_lock` (`payments.rb:14,33-34`). Rails' `with_lock` is `transaction { lock!; yield }`, and a raise inside it rolls the transaction back. The void and the failing of pending payments are therefore probably **not committed**. The context report says the void is committed; I believe that is wrong.

FE consequence: after that 422, `GET /api/invoices/:id` still returns `status.slug == "open"` until the hourly lifecycle job runs. The pay page must not refetch to learn the expiry. It has to keep a local `expired` state after that 422, or a reload makes the invoice look payable again.

This is reasoned from the code, not run. Tell the backend owner. A fix is to raise outside the lock, or call `void!` in its own transaction. This does not block the FE.

#### Safepay redirect

**Navigation**
- `ExternalNavigation.assign(url)` uses `inject(DOCUMENT).defaultView.location.assign`. Never use `router.navigate`, and never `window.open`: a popup blocker kills it after an async POST, and a new tab breaks the return-stash and session assumptions.
- Use `assign`, not `replace`. Back from Safepay then returns to the invoice the host was paying, which is the natural target. With `replace`, back lands on billing and the context is lost.

**URL validation before navigating**
- Require `new URL(checkout_url).protocol === 'https:'` and a hostname that is `getsafepay.com` or ends in `.getsafepay.com`.
- Otherwise, or when `checkout_url` is null, show the generic "couldn't start the payment" error. This guards against an open redirect from a bad or compromised response. Both backend checkout hosts satisfy it (`client.rb:10`).

**In-flight guard**
- A `starting` signal makes `hh-button [loading] [disabled]` and turns any re-entry into a no-op.
- On success it **stays true**, because the page is unloading.
- On error it resets and the server's message is shown inline.

**Retrying is safe**
- The same POST reuses the pending tracker and mints a fresh `checkout_url` (`payments.rb:60-67`). Retry, double-click and Try again are all just that POST again.
- A tracker whose payment is already `failed` gets a new tracker.

**Back from Safepay (bfcache)**
- If the browser restores the SPA from bfcache, `starting` is still true and the button spins forever.
- `SafepayCheckout.restored$` = `fromEvent<PageTransitionEvent>(window, 'pageshow').pipe(filter(e => e.persisted))`, guarded for SSR. Pages subscribe, reset `starting`, and **refetch the invoice**, since the host may have paid in the meantime.
- Without bfcache the app boots fresh on the pay URL and refetches anyway.
- jsdom cannot produce `persisted`, so a unit test dispatches a synthetic `pageshow` event with `persisted: true`.

**Return stash**
- Just before `assign`, write `sessionStorage['hh.billing.return'] = JSON.stringify({ invoiceId, hostelId })`.
- `sessionStorage` is per tab and per origin and survives a same-tab round trip through another origin.
- Wrap it in try/catch for private mode and quota errors.

##### Success page polling

**Timing**
- `pollInvoice` = `timer(0, 2500)` with `exhaustMap` to `api.invoice(id)`, so a slow request never overlaps the next one.
- Each request is caught to `{error}` so the stream survives.
- It runs `takeUntil(timer(60_000))` and `takeUntilDestroyed`.
- Use `exhaustMap`, not `switchMap`: on a slow network `switchMap` would cancel every request and never get an answer.

**Stop conditions (first match wins)**
1. `status.slug === 'paid'` → **Paid**.
2. `status.slug === 'void'` → **Not payable** ("This invoice can no longer be paid"), with Back to billing.
3. The latest `safepay` payment is `failed` **and** it is newer than the payment that was pending when polling began → **Didn't go through**, with Try again (same POST → redirect).
 - Simpler and adequate: the latest payment overall is a failed Safepay payment.
 - The failure happens through the `payment.failed` webhook. Safepay normally keeps a declined host on its own page, so this case is rare.
4. Error 403 → inline "You don't have permission to view this invoice" (our copy; the server's 403 text is generic, as in #95's checkout).
5. Error 404 → "We couldn't find this invoice". Stop.
6. Error with status 0, 5xx or a timeout → **keep polling silently**. If the cap is reached without one success, show Timed out (below) with an extra network hint when the last error was a network one.
7. 60 seconds elapsed → **Timed out**.

**What each outcome shows**
- **Confirming:** "Confirming your payment…" with a spinner or skeleton. It also shows the `hh-invoice-summary` once the first GET lands.
- **Paid:** an ok pill reading "Payment received", plan name, total, "Paid on {date}" (date only, D4), and the `hh-invoice-summary`. There is one primary button, **Go to billing** → `/<locale>/host/<hostelId>/subscription`, which refetches `/billing`. No automatic redirect: the host should see confirmation.
- **Timed out:** "We're confirming your payment. Safepay hasn't confirmed it yet. You'll get an email receipt once it's through, usually within half an hour. You don't need to pay again." Buttons: **Check again** (restarts the 60-second poll) and **Go to billing**.

The "half an hour" figure: the reconcile job checks attempts at 15 minutes or older, every 10 minutes, so about 15–25 minutes.

**The return page never decides success.** Only `status.slug === 'paid'` shows Paid. A page reached directly by URL just polls.

**Hostel for "Go to billing"**, in priority order:
1. The stash entry, when its `invoiceId` matches.
2. `HostPropertyStore.selected()` (localStorage, synchronous, no load needed).
3. `/host`, whose root redirect picks the first hostel.

A wrong guess only affects the onward link, never the payment. Clear the stash once the invoice is paid or void.

##### Cancel page

- One `GET` (no polling) with `hh-invoice-summary`.
- **open** → "Payment cancelled. You weren't charged. Invoice {number} is still open." Buttons: **Try again** (`SafepayCheckout.start` with the same invoice and the stashed hostel; same in-flight guard and 422 handling as the pay step) and **Back to billing**.
- **paid** → the Paid view. This is possible if the host paid in another tab, or the webhook landed first.
- **void** → the Not payable view.
- Try again 422s, shown verbatim:
- "This invoice has expired" → the expired view: "Choose a plan again" → billing. Keep it as local state; do not refetch, because of the rollback finding above.
- "Invoice HH-… is paid/void" → refetch, and the status view takes over.
- "Online payment is unavailable right now…" → inline, button re-enabled.
- 403 and 404 render as on the success page.

#### Edge cases and failure modes

- **Double click or a slow POST:** the `starting` guard, plus the backend reusing the tracker under `with_lock`.
- **Back from Safepay:** handled by the `pageshow`/`persisted` reset and refetch. Without bfcache there is a fresh boot and a fresh GET.
- **Host pays, webhook missing (the sandbox case documented in PR #27):** the poll times out, the copy says it is still confirming, and Check again works. The reconcile job settles it in about 15–25 minutes. This is an **expected** outcome in sandbox acceptance, not a defect.
- **Session expired while on Safepay:** `authGuard` → login → `returnUrl` with `?invoice=` → the poll resumes. A 401 mid-poll goes to the global `UNAUTHORIZED_HANDLER`.
- **Paying in another tab, or the webhook arriving before the return:** the first GET already returns paid, so the page shows Paid immediately.
- **Invoice expired while the host was on Safepay** (24-hour TTL against a 1-hour passport): the charge is flagged for refund, and the next pay-step visit shows the "charged, contact us" notice. Rare.
- **Return URL for a different user's invoice:** 403 or 404 inline. No leak, because the FE never renders anything without a successful GET.
- **Hostel mismatch:** the pay route's hostel A cannot be checked against invoice B, because the invoice has no `hostel_id`. Only the Back link is affected. Fixed by Q1's backend field.
- **D4 (UTC):** `paid_at` and the period render in UTC until the backend change lands. Use date-only formats on the new surfaces ("Paid on 11 Oct 2026"), so a UTC date is wrong by at most one day around midnight. Keep the existing "Pay by" with its time, for consistency with the payment-due box.
- **Background tab:** browsers throttle timers. The 60-second cap is by `timer`, so a backgrounded tab may stop after fewer than 25 requests. Check again covers it.
- **Zoneless:** `toSignal`/`takeUntilDestroyed` on `DestroyRef`; no `afterNextRender`. Signals update OnPush views without zone help.
- **SSR:** `pageshow`, `sessionStorage` and `location` are all guarded by `isPlatformBrowser` or `defaultView`. The return routes are client-only anyway. The pay step sits under `host`, which is client-only.
- **Unknown payment or invoice slugs** fall to neutral, with no Pay for an unknown invoice status.
- **Empty `lines`:** show only the total row.

#### Technical requirements

1. No arithmetic on amounts. Every amount goes through `billingMoney` inside `<bdi>`, and ids are used as returned.
2. Every new call is sent with `SUPPRESS_ERROR_TOAST`, and every failure is rendered inline.
3. `checkout_url` is only ever navigated to through `ExternalNavigation.assign`, and only after the https + `getsafepay.com` check passes.

#### Recommended implementation approach

- Treat the invoice as the only source of truth. Every page is "GET the invoice, render its status". The POST response's `invoice` can replace local state, but navigation happens right after it anyway.
- Put the redirect in `SafepayCheckout` once. Neither page touches `window`, `sessionStorage` or `pageshow` directly.
- Return pages are thin and top-level, keyed by invoice only. The hostel is a best-effort onward link (stash → `selected()` → `/host`), never a precondition.
- Polling is a pure, separately tested RxJS function using `exhaustMap`. The page only maps ticks to a view union, following #95's `BillingView` pattern (`billing-page.ts:55-60`).
- **Avoid:**
- `window.open`.
- `switchMap` polling.
- Auto-redirecting after paid.
- Refetching after "This invoice has expired".
- Rendering `failure_reason` or the server's 403 text.
- A `/billing` fetch on the pay step just to read `payment_methods`.
- Moving `PaymentDue` or other #95 code.
- `afterNextRender`.

#### 1. Problem, intent and user impact

**Problem.** Since #95, every Choose plan, Renew, Upgrade and Pay click ends on a stub that says "Online payment is being set up". Hosts get real invoices they cannot pay. #95's D2 holds the production release until #96 ships, so this card is what lets billing go live.

**Intent:**
- Before leaving HostelHive, the host sees exactly what they are paying for. Safepay's page shows only an amount, with no merchant name and no line items.
- The host is then handed to Safepay with one click.
- On return, the host learns the truth about the payment. That truth is the invoice status from the server, never the return URL.

Every exit must leave the host with a clear next step and no fear of being charged twice.

| Party | Situation | What they experience after #96 |
|---|---|---|
| **Owner paying a new invoice** | First plan, right after creating a hostel | Invoice summary ("First payment · period"), one **Pay PKR 5,000** button, then Safepay, then "Confirming…", then "Payment received". Billing then shows the plan as active. |
| **Owner renewing** (incl. past-due) | Renewal invoice from checkout or the payment-due box | Same flow, with "Renewal · period". |
| **Owner upgrading** | Prorated upgrade invoice | Same flow, with "Upgrade charge · period". The total is the server's prorated amount, never derived. |
| **Host back from Safepay, webhook fast** | Typical case | Confirmation within seconds, then success. |
| **Host back, confirmation slow** (sandbox often; production when a webhook is missed) | Poll times out after about 60 s | A calm "still confirming" message: no need to pay again, a notification follows, usually minutes and at most about 30. |
| **Host who cancelled on Safepay** | Cancel return | "Payment not completed" and **Try again**, which takes them to Safepay again for the same invoice. |
| **Host whose invoice expired** | Pay → 422 "This invoice has expired" (the server voids it) | Message shown as returned, plus **Choose a plan again**, which goes to the billing screen. |
| **Host whose invoice is already paid** (another tab, a teammate, a slow webhook that has now landed) | Payment step or 422 "Invoice HH-… is paid" | The page switches to a "paid" state. No Pay button. |

#### 2. Acceptance criteria

##### A. Payment step (`/host/:id/subscription/pay/:invoiceId`, replacing `PayPlaceholder`, same URL per #95 D1)

1. **Loading.** Given a host opens the payment step, the page calls `GET /api/invoices/:id` every time, using router-state data only as a prefill while the request is in flight. A skeleton shows until it answers.
2. **Summary of an open invoice.** Given `status.slug == "open"`, the page shows:
 - "Invoice {number}";
 - the plan name (`invoice.plan.name`, as returned);
 - the purpose line from `kind` (the existing `kindNew` / `kindRenewal` / `kindUpgrade` with `period_start`–`period_end`; an unknown kind shows the period only);
 - each `lines[]` row as `description` plus `amount` through `billingMoney` with the invoice `currency`. Descriptions appear exactly as returned (server English);
 - a **Total** row showing `invoice.total` through `billingMoney`. It is never a sum of the lines;
 - "Pay by {expires_at}" (`d MMM yyyy, h:mm a`, via `apiDate`).
3. **Provider notice.** Given an open invoice, the page shows the copy `hostPayment.providerNotice` and `hostPayment.providerAmountOnly` (section 5) above the button.
4. **One button.** Given an open invoice, there is exactly one primary button, **Pay {total}** (for example "Pay PKR 5,000" or "Pay $18"). There is no method picker, no bank-transfer option and no bank details, even though `billing.payment_methods` lists `bank_transfer`.
5. **Redirect.**
 - Given Pay is clicked, the page sends `POST /api/invoices/:id/payments {payment_method:"safepay"}` with the id exactly as returned, using `SUPPRESS_ERROR_TOAST`.
 - On 201 the browser goes to `checkout_url` with a full-page navigation in the same tab.
 - The page never builds or changes `checkout_url`.
6. **In flight.**
 - Given the request is in flight, Pay is disabled, shows a spinner and reads "Taking you to Safepay…".
 - Repeated clicks send no second request.
 - The button stays disabled after 201 until the browser leaves.
7. **Back button from Safepay.**
 - Given the host presses Back on Safepay and the browser restores the page from its back/forward cache, Pay is enabled again. It is not stuck in "Taking you to Safepay…".
 - Clicking it again sends the same POST. The server reuses the pending session, so this is safe.
8. **No `checkout_url`.** Given a 201 with a null or empty `checkout_url`, the page shows `hostPayment.startFailed` inline and re-enables Pay. It does not navigate.
9. **422 shown as returned.** Given the POST returns 422, `errors[0]` appears word for word inline under the button, with no toast, and the buttons are re-enabled where noted:

 | Server message | Extra behaviour |
 |---|---|
 | "This invoice has expired" | Pay is replaced by **Choose a plan again**, which goes to `/host/:id/subscription`. The invoice is voided server-side, so the page does not offer Pay again. |
 | "Invoice HH-… is paid" | The page re-fetches the invoice and switches to the Paid state (AC 11). |
 | "Invoice HH-… is void" | The page re-fetches and switches to the Void state (AC 12). |
 | "There is nothing to pay on invoice HH-…" | Pay is hidden. **Back to billing** is shown. |
 | "Online payment is unavailable right now, please try again (…)" | Pay is re-enabled, so the host can retry. |
 | "Safepay isn't available for … invoices", or any other 422 | Message as returned. Pay is re-enabled. |
10. **Other errors.**
  - A 403 on the POST shows our copy `hostPayment.payForbidden`, never the server text. This is consistent with #95's checkout 403 decision.
  - A network error or 5xx shows `hostPayment.startFailed`, and Pay is re-enabled.
11. **Paid invoice.** Given `status.slug == "paid"` on load, the page shows:
  - the summary without Pay;
  - a pill reading "Paid";
  - "Paid on {paid_at}";
  - **Go to billing**.
12. **Void invoice.** Given `status.slug == "void"`, the page shows:
  - the number and the total;
  - `hostPayment.voidTitle` / `voidMessage`;
  - **Choose a plan again**, which goes to billing.

  There is no Pay button.
13. **Unknown status.** Given an unknown slug, the page shows the summary, a neutral pill with `status.name`, no Pay, and Back to billing.
14. **Expiry decided by the server.** Given an open invoice whose `expires_at` is in the past by the browser clock, Pay is **still shown**, and the server's 422 decides. No client-side expiry logic.
15. **Pending attempt.** Given the latest entry in `invoice.payments[]` is a `safepay` payment with `status.slug == "pending"`, the page shows the soft notice `hostPayment.pendingNotice` above Pay. Pay stays enabled.
16. **Failed attempt.** Given the latest payment is `failed` and the invoice is still open, the page shows `hostPayment.lastAttemptFailed`. It **never** shows `failure_reason`. Pay stays enabled. Older attempts are not listed (that belongs to #97).
17. **Load errors.**
  - A 403 on the GET shows the inline "no access" state, reusing `hostBilling.noAccessTitle` / `noAccessMessage`.
  - A 404 shows `hostPayment.notFoundTitle` / `notFoundMessage` and Back to billing.
  - Any other failure shows `hostBilling.invoiceLoadError`, Try again, and Back to billing.
18. **No payment permission.**
  - Given the session's permission flags are loaded and lack `core:Invoice:create_payment`, Pay is replaced by `hostPayment.payForbidden` (the "ask the hostel owner" text).
  - If the flags are absent (`hasPermission` fails open), Pay shows, and a 403 is handled by AC 10.
19. **RTL.** Given ar or ur, the layout mirrors, and each amount stays one bidi unit (`<bdi>`), the same as #95.

##### B. Return page: success (`/billing/payment/success?invoice=:id`)

20. **Routing.** Given a browser lands on the success URL, with or without a locale prefix:
  - the route exists. It does not fall through to `**` and lose the query;
  - it is client-rendered;
  - it is behind `authGuard`;
  - it renders in the host's remembered language even when the URL is bare.
21. **Signed out.** Given the host is signed out, they go to login and come back to the same URL with `?invoice=` intact. Confirmation then starts.
22. **Missing parameter.** Given `invoice` is missing or empty, the page shows `hostPayment.missingInvoiceTitle` / `Message` and **Go to billing**. It does not poll.
23. **Confirming.** Given a valid id, the page shows "Confirming your payment…" with a spinner. It calls `GET /api/invoices/:id` immediately, then every 2–3 s, until one of the following happens:
  - `status.slug == "paid"`: the Success state (AC 24);
  - `status.slug == "void"`: the Void-after-payment state (AC 26);
  - about 60 s pass: the Timeout state (AC 25);
  - a 403 or 404: the no-access or not-found state (same copy as AC 17).

  A transient network error or 5xx during polling does not end the poll. It counts toward the 60 s.
24. **Success.** Given `paid`, the page shows:
  - "Payment received";
  - "Invoice {number} is paid";
  - the plan name with the kind and period line;
  - the amount;
  - **Go to billing**, which goes to the hostel's billing screen (Q1). The billing screen re-fetches `/billing`; nothing is updated optimistically.

  The page shows the success heading **only** after the server returns `paid`. No copy anywhere says "successful" before that.
25. **Timeout.** Given about 60 s without `paid`, the page:
  - stops polling;
  - shows `hostPayment.slowTitle` and `slowMessage`, which says not to pay again, that a notification will follow, and that this usually takes minutes and at most about 30 minutes;
  - offers **Check again** (one more polling round) and **Go to billing**.

  It never says the payment failed, and never offers Pay.
26. **Void after payment.** Given `void` during polling, the page shows `hostPayment.voidAfterPayTitle` / `Message` (the invoice can't be paid; contact us if you were charged, quoting the number), plus Contact us and Go to billing. This covers the server's flag-for-refund case without showing its internal wording.
27. **Leaving the page.** Given the host leaves during polling, polling stops. There are no background requests.

##### C. Return page: cancel (`/billing/payment/cancel?invoice=:id`)

28. **Routing.** Same as AC 20–22: the route exists, it is client-only, it uses `authGuard`, the query survives login, and a missing parameter shows the same state as AC 22.
29. **Open invoice.** The page fetches the invoice once (no polling). Given it is open, it shows:
  - "Payment not completed";
  - `hostPayment.cancelMessage`;
  - the invoice number and total;
  - **Try again**;
  - **Back to billing**.
30. **Try again.** Given Try again is clicked, the page sends the same `POST …/payments {payment_method:"safepay"}` with the same in-flight, back-cache and 422 behaviour as AC 5–10, and redirects to the new `checkout_url`.
31. **Invoice no longer payable.**
  - Given the fetched invoice is `paid`, the page shows the Success state (AC 24). The host may have paid in another tab.
  - Given it is `void`, the page shows the Void state with **Choose a plan again**.
  - Given Try again returns "This invoice has expired", that message is shown as returned, plus **Choose a plan again**.
32. **Load errors.** A 403 or 404 shows the same inline states as AC 17.

##### D. Everywhere

33. **Server messages.** No page shows `failure_reason`, `gateway_reference`, a tracker id, or the server's 403 text.
34. **Toasts.** Every billing call on these pages sends `SUPPRESS_ERROR_TOAST`, so there is never an inline error plus a toast.
35. **Permission helper.** `isSubscriptionError` is never used here (#95 D13).
36. **Release.** Once #96 merges, #95's D2 release hold is lifted.

#### 6. Product risks and open questions

##### Risks

- **P1. Return URLs are deploy configuration.**
- `SAFEPAY_SUCCESS_URL` and `SAFEPAY_CANCEL_URL` are set per environment by the backend owner, and nothing in the backend adds a locale.
- If they don't point at `/billing/payment/success|cancel` on the right frontend origin, every paying host lands on the homepage.
- This blocks the acceptance test and the release. Hamza must confirm the dev and production values.
- **P2. Sandbox timeouts.** PR #27 recorded a sandbox payment that was paid but never sent a webhook. The acceptance run may end on "still confirming" even though the payment succeeded. That is AC 25 behaving correctly, not a defect. Re-check through the billing screen after the safety net runs.
- **P3. A possible double charge in the pending case.**
- If a host paid on Safepay, the webhook is late, and they press Pay again, the server reuses the same tracker.
- Whether Safepay then refuses a second charge on an ended tracker is unverified.
- AC 15's notice reduces the risk. The backend's `flag_for_refund` is the backstop.
- **P4. A technical suffix in a 422.** "Online payment is unavailable right now, please try again (Couldn't reach Safepay: …)" carries technical detail. The ground rule says show it as returned, so I recommend accepting it. Ask the backend to drop the parenthetical later if it looks bad.
- **P5. Mixed languages.** Line descriptions, plan names and 422s are English in ur and ar. This is the same accepted trade-off as #95's P3.
- **P6. Dates may still show in UTC** (D4) until the backend timezone change lands. "Pay by" is a date and time, so a wrong offset is more visible here than on the billing screen.

export interface PollInvoiceOptions { everyMs?: number; forMs?: number; until?: (tick: PollTick) => boolean; }
