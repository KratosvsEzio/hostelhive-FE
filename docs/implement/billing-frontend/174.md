### Ticket (Trello #174), in short

The full text is on the card. It replaces #93 section 10. Two new ways to pay, both through Safepay; invoices, statuses and receipts are unchanged.

- **A. Saved card + auto-renewal.**
  - Save or replace a card (POST `…/subscription/card` → redirect; `?card=saved|canceled`).
  - Remove it (DELETE, which also turns auto-renew off).
  - Auto-renew toggle (PUT `…/auto_renew {enabled}`; 422 without a card).
  - Pay an invoice and keep the card (`{safepay, save_card:true}`).
  - Pay now with the saved card (`{saved_card}`, no redirect; 422 "Your card was declined: …").
- **B. Safepay Subscriptions, with a one-time free trial.**
  - POST `…/subscription/safepay {plan_id}` → `{checkout_url, trial_days}` → `?subscription=started|canceled`.
  - A Safepay-billed plan shows "Billed monthly by Safepay" and, during a trial (`TRAILING`), "Free trial until …".
  - It hides upgrade, downgrade, saved card and auto-renew; Cancel/Resume stay.
- **Also:**
  - Renewal and grace copy with auto-renew.
  - Return pages routed on `invoice` / `card` / `subscription`.
  - Invoice detail labels `initiated_by` (`schedule` → "Charged automatically", `upgrade` → "Saved card").
  - Notifications `card_declined` / `card_expiring`, using the backend's text.
  - Every 422 shown as returned.

### Backend contract (from code, `origin/main` @ `d2b827a`; matches the card except where noted)

- **Billing JSON** (`Billing::Summary#as_json`, `summary.rb:10-22`):
  - `auto_renew`: true only while there is a saved card, no `cancel_at_period_end`, and the plan is not Safepay-billed (`subscription.rb:37`).
  - `saved_card`: null or `{label, expires_on}`.
  - `payment_methods`: `"saved_card"` is appended when a card is saved and safepay is offered.
  - `subscription.billed_by`: `"invoice" | "safepay_subscription"`.
  - `subscription.safepay_status`: the raw Safepay status (`"TRAILING"` = trial).
  - `plans[].trial_days`: null when a plan is live, the trial is already used, or Safepay is unconfigured.
  - `payments[].initiated_by`.
- **Safepay-billed plan:** every plan card is `action: "unavailable"` with reason "This plan is billed by Safepay: cancel it, then subscribe to the new plan", the current plan included. `schedule_downgrade` is refused.
- **Card save and subscription start are asynchronous** (webhook, or an hourly sync), so the `?card=saved` and `?subscription=started` pages must poll `GET …/billing`. The card's flows say every 2–3 s for up to 60 s.
- **`save_card` on an invoice** returns `?invoice=` as before; the card appears later in billing.
- **Notifications:** `UserInvite` types `card_declined` (17) and `card_expiring` (18), `actionable:false`, with server-built messages:
  - "We couldn't charge your card for invoice %{number} (%{hostel_name})"
  - "The card for %{hostel_name}'s automatic renewal expires on %{date}"

  The frontend already shows unknown types read-only, with a title-cased label and a bell icon.
- **422 messages:** as in the card, plus "Card payments aren't available for <CUR> invoices", "This invoice has expired", "Invoice <n> is paid|void", "There is nothing to pay on invoice <n>", "There is no active subscription".

  - `returnParam()` strips `?tracker=` for every param.
  - `payErrorOutcome` gains `declined` and `inProgress`, and the regex matches "aren't available for".

**A: saved card and auto-renew**
- **T1, billing page:**
  - A new `saved-card.ts` block: label; expiry (`MM/yy`; warns within 30 days or when expired); Save / Replace / Remove (with confirm).
  - An auto-renew switch: server-driven, reverted on failure, disabled while cancelling. It is never inferred from `saved_card`.
  - Current-plan card: "Renews automatically on …".
  - Payment due: "We'll charge {card} on {date}. You can also pay now."
- **T2, payment step:**
  - "Save this card and renew my plan automatically" checkbox: unticked by default, hidden when a Safepay checkout is already pending.
  - "Pay {amount} with {card}" when `payment_methods` includes `saved_card`. It opens a confirm dialog (the card text says to), then:
    - `succeeded`: show paid;
    - `pending`: poll `refresh`;
    - decline 422: shown verbatim, with Update card and Pay with another card.
- **T3, return pages:** a dispatcher routes on `invoice` / `card` / `subscription`. The invoice pages are unchanged. The new return component polls billing:
  - a card is done when `saved_card` differs from the previous one;
  - a subscription is done when `isSafepayBilled`.

  States: confirming, saved/started, slow (honest "can take up to an hour" with Check again), canceled, missing, forbidden, error.
- **T4, banners:**
  - Auto-renew variants: renewing "…we'll charge your saved card" with no Renew; renew-due "charging your card"; past-due with Update card + Pay now.
  - Safepay-billed variants: no Renew.
  - The banner component gains a second action.
- **T5:** `initiated_by` labels on payment attempts (`schedule` → "Charged automatically", `upgrade` → "Saved card"). One shared notification-type map (it is duplicated in the page and the bell today), with `card_declined` and `card_expiring` using `ti-credit-card` and the server's message verbatim.

**B: Safepay Subscriptions (T6)**
- `buy` plan cards: secondary "Start {n}-day free trial" when `trial_days > 0`, otherwise "Subscribe through Safepay". "Choose plan" stays primary.
- Live Safepay-billed:
  - "Current plan" on its own plan, "Unavailable" on the rest, with the server reason shown once above the grid.
  - "Billed monthly by Safepay", or "Free trial until …" when `TRAILING`.
  - Card, auto-renew, upgrade and downgrade controls hidden; Cancel and Resume kept, with trial-specific confirm copy.
- An open invoice gets a confirm before subscribing.

**Integrator (T7):** all 18 locale files, the knowledge base (`area/host-billing-and-subscription.md`, which is stale) and the final gates.

### Principal engineer report

Summarised by the orchestrator; the full report is in the session transcript.

- **Backend facts:**
  - `auto_renew` is derived (`subscription.rb`), and reads false while cancelling even after a successful PUT.
  - A saved-card charge can return 201 `pending` (Safepay timeout), which `refresh` settles.
  - A re-opened checkout keeps its first `save_card` choice, and the FE cannot see that choice.
  - A subscription goes live only on its webhook.
  - `billed_by` stays `safepay_subscription` after expiry until an invoice-paid payment, so it must be combined with a live status.
  - Every plan is `unavailable` while Safepay-billed and live (`checkout.rb:38`).
  - Card actions need a plan ("Choose a plan first").
  - Cancel and resume need `active`.
  - `expires_on` is the end-of-month date.
  - Every Safepay URL is on `*.getsafepay.com`.
- **Pitfalls:**
  - `hh-toggle` flips itself before the server answers, so bind it to a `linkedSignal` and write the server value back on error.
  - The unpayable regex misses "aren't available".
  - `ti-credit-card-off` and `ti-repeat` are not in the icon subset.
  - Notification `associated_id` is a payment or subscription id, not a hostel id.
- **Decomposition:**
  - Wave 1: T0 alone.
  - Then T1–T5 in parallel (disjoint files).
  - T6 after T1, because they share `billing-page.*` and `current-plan-card.ts`.
  - T7 last: locales and KB.
- **PR split:** A = T0–T5 + T7 (subscription return branch included, since it is the same poll); B = T6 + the Safepay banner variants and copy.

- in-app navigation to `#saved-card`: the block lands at 64 px and stays;
- full reload: 64 px, one billing fetch per consumer, no layout-shift entries;
- 9 s of sampling after load: no movement.

The one mechanism that moves the block is a billing refetch: the page swaps to the loading skeleton (scroll height 1085 → 737), the scroller resets to 0, and the block returns at 470 px. A refetch only runs on Retry, a stale (409/422) plan or card change, or a checkout refused for a pending transfer or 404, each right after a user action that shows a message. That does not match the 200 px move, which most likely came from the test timing (the card had just synced and the tab was still on the splash). No change was made. The optional hardening is to keep the loaded content on screen during a refetch instead of the skeleton.
