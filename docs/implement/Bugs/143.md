### The cards

- **#143:** `RouteHeadStrategy.updateTitle` (`core/route-head.ts`) sets the route `title:` synchronously on every navigation, because Google Analytics reads `document.title` a tick after `NavigationEnd`. On `'page'` routes the page's own `Seo.apply` effect overwrites it, but only when that effect re-runs. Three gaps:
  1. **Query-only navigation on a `'page'` route that has a route title.** Listing, pricing and blog have one. Closing reviews on a listing (`review: null`, `listing-detail.ts` ~946) leaves "Hostel details — HostelHive".
  2. **The listing applies its noindex not-found head while it loads,** so the tab and GA's page view record the not-found title.
  3. **The search routes have no `title:`,** so GA's synchronous read records the previous page's title.

  **AC:**
  - On a listing, opening and closing reviews keeps the listing's own title.
  - GA page views for search and listing carry that page's title, not the previous page's and not a not-found title.
- **#140:** route `title:` strings, about 71 across `app.routes.ts` and 9 feature route files (admin, auth, host, payment return, subscription, moderator, blog, listing, onboarding), are English literals. So `/ur/about` has the tab and share title "About Us — HostelHive". The blog index's `Seo.apply` title and description (`blog.ts`) are English literals too.

  **AC:**
  - `/ur/about` has an Urdu `<title>` and `og:title`; `/en/about` is unchanged.
  - `/ur/blog` has an Urdu title and description.

  Fix direction: translate through Transloco, starting with the public indexable pages; console titles matter less (noindex). New strings go in all 18 locale files.

### What we already decided in this area

- **#132 (PR #104):** `RouteHeadStrategy` owns the head of `'index'` and unflagged routes through a root effect gated on `locale.ready()`.
  - `'page'` routes own their head through `Seo.apply` in a `ready()`-gated effect.
  - The route title is set **synchronously** for GA (D8).
- **D7:** translating titles was deferred to #140. Descriptions of the `'index'` pages are already translated through `descriptionKey`.
- **Who-owns-the-head KB:** "a route `title:` on a `'page'` route can outlive the page's title" is the recorded trap (#143).
- **Rejected in #132:** applying the whole default head synchronously in `updateTitle`, because on a language switch the new strings may not be loaded. **This bears directly on translating the synchronous title.**

### Understanding

#140 needs the route title to become a translation key, resolved in the active language. That collides with D8: the title is set synchronously, before a new language's strings may have loaded. So the design question is how GA gets a correct, translated title.

#143 asks that a `'page'` route's own title never be clobbered by the route title, and that GA records the page's real title. Both point at the same mechanism: **who sets `<title>`, when, and in which language.** Expect the plan to move title ownership for `'page'` routes fully into the page (the route title becomes only a fallback, or is removed), and to make the default-route title a translated key.

**I understand the ticket.** The open design questions go to the interview (2d): how GA reads the title (synchronous vs deferred), and whether console titles are translated now or later.

## Root cause (confirmed in Phase 5: every fix row fails on main and passes on the branch)

**Router order** (zoneless scheduler, `@angular/core` 21.2.9): the change-detection tick is registered at activation (`_router-chunk.mjs:3883`, through `scheduleCallbackWithRafRace`). GA registers its `setTimeout` at `NavigationEnd` (`:3893`), which is later. So every root and page effect runs **before** GA reads `document.title`. GA does **not** wait for `ready()` or for data.

**D8 of #132 rested on a wrong assumption:** that GA needs the title set synchronously in `updateTitle`.

### Found while reading GA: the prefix bug, escalated

- **Cause:** `isMarketplacePath` (`google-analytics.service.ts:41-45`) compares unprefixed paths. Since every URL gained a locale prefix (`fbe7625`), `/en/host/…`, `/en/admin`, `/en/reset_password?…` and `/en/confirm_invitation?…` all pass as **marketplace** pages.
- **Effect:** `track()` and `sendPageView` send them with the full `page_location`, **including any token in the query**.
- **Why it escaped:** `google-analytics.spec.ts:26-38` tests unprefixed URLs only.
- **Impact:** only where a `measurementId` is configured, and only after consent. It is empty locally, and `google-analytics.env.ts` is generated, so the production value is unknown here.
- **Fix:** `isMarketplacePath(routePath(url))`, plus prefixed spec rows.

## Plan

### Merged plan (the PE's recommended option (a), extended)

**One rule: the title is part of the head.** Only `Seo.apply` writes it, translated, in the `ready()`-gated effect of whoever owns the head. D8 is superseded.

1. **`Seo`** (`core/seo.ts`) gains `SeoConfig.provisional?: boolean` and `readonly settled = computed(() => locale.ready() && !provisional)`. Every `apply` resets `provisional` from its config.
2. **`RouteHeadStrategy`** (`core/route-head.ts`) stops calling `setTitle`.
   - On `'page'` routes it ignores the title entirely.
   - On default routes, `pending.title` is a **key**. `applyDefault` composes the title from `seo.pageTitle` (`"{{page}} — HostelHive"`), or `seo.adminPageTitle` under `/admin`, with the translated label.
3. **Route titles.**
   - Remove `title:` from the four `'page'` routes (home, pricing, blog, listing).
   - Turn the other 67 literals into **label keys**. About 50 reuse existing translated labels present in all 18 files (for example `footer.privacyPolicy`, `publicAbout.aboutUs`, `common.faqs`, `hostRooms.addRoom`).
   - About 16 new labels are needed.
4. **Listing** (`listing-detail.ts:989-1007`):
   - While loading: apply a provisional, noindex, translated "Hostel details — HostelHive" head.
   - On error or a real not-found: keep the not-found head as final.
   - Served HTML when a server render is cut off is unchanged.
5. **Blog index:** the title and description go through `seo.blogTitle` and `seo.blogDescription`.
6. **GA** (`google-analytics.service.ts`): after `NavigationEnd`, wait one macrotask, then for `seo.settled()`, then one more macrotask, and read `document.title`.
   - **Cap: 5 s.**
   - **Flush** at the next `NavigationEnd` and on `pagehide`.
   - Exactly one `page_view` per navigation. Optional: dedupe and the prefix fix (questions for the interview).
7. **Locale files:** about 20 new keys across 18 files, translated in session as in the precedents `e60bcf1` and `688b1fe`, plus a new all-18-files parity spec (the precedent is `billing-locale-parity.spec.ts`).
8. **Specs:**
   - `route-head.spec` U11 and U17 are **rewritten on purpose** (they encode D8).
   - `app.routes.spec` gains two checks: no `'page'` route has a title, and every title is a key.

## Summary

**Area:** SSR, SEO and head tags (`ssr-seo-and-prerender`), i18n, analytics.

**What changed:**
- **The title is part of the head (D1, supersedes #132's D8).**
  - `RouteHeadStrategy` (`core/route-head.ts`) no longer writes `<title>`.
  - Pages that own their head (home, search, landing, listing, pricing, blog) own their title completely.
  - Every other route's `title:` is now a **translation key**, composed into `seo.pageTitle` ("{{page}} — HostelHive") or `seo.adminPageTitle` under `/<locale>/admin`, in the active language, re-applied on a language switch.
  - **The four `'page'` routes no longer declare titles,** which fixes the stale title after a query-only navigation (#143 gap 1).
- **Translations (#140):** 67 route titles map to keys. 52 reuse labels already translated in all 18 files; 15 new `routeTitle.*` labels and 5 new `seo.*` keys were added, translated in all 18 languages. The blog index's title and description are translated.
- **English wording changes (D5/D7):**
  - "Service Policy" becomes "Service policy";
  - "Access blocked" becomes "Access blocked — HostelHive";
  - "Host · Overview" becomes "Overview — HostelHive".

  Every other English title is byte-identical.
- **Listing (#143 gap 2, D6, D17):** loading shows a neutral, translated "Hostel details — HostelHive", noindex and *provisional*. An API error or outage shows the same title as final. "Listing not found" appears only on a genuine 404.
- **`Seo`:** extended with `SeoConfig.provisional` and `Seo.settled`.
- **Google Analytics (`core/google-analytics/google-analytics.service.ts`):**
  - **Wait for the head:** `page_view` now waits until the head has settled (strings loaded, no provisional head), then one more macrotask, before reading the title. That fixes the real form of gap 3, a language switch recording the previous language's title.
  - **Cap and flush:** the wait is capped at 5 s. A pending view is flushed on the next navigation and on `pagehide`, carrying its own location. Each navigation sends exactly one view.
