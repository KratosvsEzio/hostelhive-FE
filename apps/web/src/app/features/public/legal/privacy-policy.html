Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 August 2026 · Last updated: 13 August 2026

HostelHive ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the personal data of every individual who uses our platform. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and your rights under applicable Pakistani law — including the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act 2016 (PECA), the Electronic Transactions Ordinance 2002 (ETO), and the principles outlined in the Personal Data Protection Bill.

By creating an account or using hostelhive.com (the "Platform"), you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please discontinue use of the Platform immediately.

1. Definitions

  • "Personal Data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, including name, email, phone number, CNIC number, photographs, and location data.
  • "Seeker" means a user who browses or books hostel accommodation through the Platform.
  • "Host" means a user who lists, manages, or operates hostel accommodation through the Platform.
  • "Processing" includes collection, recording, storage, retrieval, use, disclosure, transfer, or deletion of Personal Data.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account registration: full name, email address, phone number, and password (hashed; we never store plaintext passwords).
  • Host onboarding: hostel name, address, CNIC or business registration number, bank account details for payouts, and property photographs.
  • Profile updates: profile picture, gender preference, and communication preferences.
  • Correspondence: messages you send through our support channels or in-app communication.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device and browser data: IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device identifiers, and screen resolution.
  • Usage data: pages visited, search queries, click events, session duration, and referral URLs.
  • Location data: approximate geographic location derived from your IP address or, with your explicit consent, precise GPS location on mobile devices.
  • Cookies and local storage: authentication tokens (stored as HTTP-only cookies), session identifiers, and user preferences. See Section 8 for details.

2.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Google OAuth: if you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account name, email address, and profile picture. We do not receive or store your Google password.
  • Payment processors: transaction status and reference IDs. We do not store full card numbers, CVVs, or bank PINs on our servers.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your Personal Data on the following grounds, in accordance with Pakistani law:

  • Contractual necessity: to perform our obligations under the Terms of Service you accepted (Contract Act 1872, Sections 2–10).
  • Legitimate interest: to improve the Platform, prevent fraud, ensure safety, and enforce our policies.
  • Consent: where you have given explicit consent, such as for marketing emails or precise location access. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation: where we are required by law to retain or disclose data, including compliance with PECA 2016 or orders from the Pakistan Telecommunication Authority (PTA), Federal Investigation Agency (FIA), or a court of competent jurisdiction.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account, authenticate your identity, and maintain session security.
  • To display hostel listings, process search queries, and show location-relevant results.
  • To process subscription payments for Hosts and generate invoices and billing records.
  • To send transactional communications: booking confirmations, payment receipts, account verification emails, and password reset links.
  • To send marketing communications (only with your opt-in consent) about new features, listings, or promotions. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, or security incidents in compliance with PECA 2016.
  • To comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests from Pakistani authorities.
  • To generate anonymised, aggregated analytics to improve the Platform (no individual is identifiable from such data).

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your Personal Data. In accordance with PECA 2016, Section 38, we do not transfer or disclose your Personal Data to any person without lawful authority. We may share it in the following limited circumstances:

  • Between Seekers and Hosts: when a Seeker views or inquires about a listing, the Host may see the Seeker's name and contact information necessary to facilitate the accommodation.
  • Service providers: trusted third-party vendors who perform services on our behalf (email delivery, payment processing, cloud hosting, analytics). These providers are contractually bound to use your data only for the services they provide to us.
  • Legal requirements: when required by a court order, subpoena, or lawful request from the FIA, PTA, or any other competent authority under Pakistani law, including cooperation with investigations under PECA 2016, Section 34 (provisions for investigation and data production).
  • Anti-money laundering: where required to fulfil our obligations under the Anti-Money Laundering Act 2010 (AMLA), including filing Suspicious Transaction Reports (STRs) with the Financial Monitoring Unit (FMU) and complying with customer due diligence requirements.
  • Business transfers: in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all assets, your data may be transferred to the successor entity. We will notify you in advance of any such transfer.
  • With your consent: in any other situation, we will share your data only with your explicit prior consent.

6. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Personal Data is retained for as long as your account remains active and for a period of one (1) year after account deletion to comply with record-keeping obligations.
  • Financial records: invoices, payment records, and subscription data are retained for six (6) years in accordance with applicable tax and commercial law (Income Tax Ordinance 2001, Companies Act 2017).
  • Traffic data: traffic data (IP addresses, access logs, connection timestamps, and session metadata) is retained for a minimum of one (1) year in compliance with PECA 2016, Section 29, which requires service providers to retain traffic data for the purpose of investigation and prosecution of offences. After the statutory retention period, traffic data is permanently deleted.
  • Anonymised data: aggregated, de-identified data that cannot reasonably be linked to any individual may be retained indefinitely for analytical purposes.

7. Data Security

We implement technical and organisational safeguards designed to protect your Personal Data from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These include:

  • All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher.
  • Passwords are hashed using industry-standard one-way cryptographic algorithms (bcrypt); plaintext passwords are never stored.
  • Authentication tokens are stored as HTTP-only, Secure cookies to prevent cross-site scripting (XSS) access.
  • Access to production systems is restricted to authorised personnel through role-based access controls.
  • Regular security assessments and vulnerability scans.

Despite these measures, no system connected to the internet can guarantee absolute security. In the event of a data breach affecting your Personal Data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of the breach, as recommended by international best practice and the draft Personal Data Protection Bill.

8. Cookies and Tracking

We use the following types of cookies:

  • Strictly necessary cookies: authentication tokens and session identifiers required for the Platform to function. These cannot be disabled.
  • Functional cookies: remember your preferences (language, theme, last-viewed listings).
  • Analytics cookies: help us understand how visitors interact with the public pages of the Platform. We use Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC) for this. IP addresses are anonymised, no personally identifying information is sent, and Google's advertising features are disabled, so you are not tracked across third-party websites.

Analytics cookies are set only if you consent. Nothing is loaded and no data is sent until you accept; declining leaves the Platform fully functional. You may change or withdraw your choice at any time from Account › Settings, or manage cookies through your browser settings. Analytics are not used in the host, moderator or admin consoles.

We do not use third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking pixels.

9. Your Rights

Under applicable Pakistani law and international best practices, you have the right to:

  • Access: request a copy of the Personal Data we hold about you.
  • Correction: request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: request deletion of your Personal Data, subject to our retention obligations. You may delete your account from the Account Settings page.
  • Restriction: request that we limit processing of your data in certain circumstances.
  • Data portability: request an export of your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent: where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
  • Complaint: lodge a complaint with the Pakistan Telecommunication Authority (PTA) or any other relevant regulatory body.

No fees. We do not charge any fee for processing your data access, correction, deletion, or portability requests. All rights listed above may be exercised free of charge.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at support@hostelhive.com. We will respond within thirty (30) days of receiving a verifiable request. If we are unable to fulfil your request (for example, due to a legal retention obligation), we will explain the reason in writing within the same timeframe.

10. Children's and Young Users' Privacy

The Platform is not intended for individuals under the age of eighteen (18). We do not knowingly collect Personal Data from minors. If we become aware that a minor has provided us with Personal Data without verifiable parental or guardian consent, we will promptly delete such data. If you believe a minor has registered on the Platform, please contact us immediately at support@hostelhive.com.

Users aged 18–21: Many of our users are university and college students. While users aged eighteen (18) and above are legally eligible to use the Platform under the Contract Act 1872, we encourage parents and guardians of younger adult users to review this Privacy Policy and to be aware of their ward's use of the Platform, particularly with respect to sharing personal information and entering into accommodation arrangements.

11. International Data Transfers

Our servers are hosted within secure data centres. If your data is transferred outside Pakistan for processing (e.g., by a cloud-hosting provider), we ensure that the recipient provides an adequate level of data protection consistent with this Privacy Policy and applicable Pakistani law.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified via email or a prominent notice on the Platform at least fifteen (15) days before they take effect. Your continued use of the Platform after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
