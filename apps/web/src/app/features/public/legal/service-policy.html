Service Policy

Effective date: 1 August 2026 · Last updated: 16 August 2026

This Service Policy describes the standards, procedures, and commitments that govern how HostelHive ("we," "us," or "our") delivers its platform services to Seekers and Hosts (collectively, "Users"). It supplements our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. In the event of any conflict between this Service Policy and the Terms of Service, the Terms of Service shall prevail.

1. Platform Availability

1.1 Uptime Commitment

We strive to maintain Platform availability of 99.5% on a monthly basis, measured across all calendar days in the month. This excludes scheduled maintenance windows and circumstances beyond our reasonable control (see Force Majeure in our Terms of Service).

1.2 Scheduled Maintenance

Planned maintenance that may affect Platform availability will be announced at least forty-eight (48) hours in advance via email notification to registered users and/or an in-app banner. Wherever possible, maintenance will be scheduled during off-peak hours (typically between 2:00 AM and 6:00 AM Pakistan Standard Time).

1.3 Unscheduled Downtime

In the event of unscheduled downtime, we will work to restore service as quickly as possible and provide status updates through our official channels. Users will not be charged for subscription periods during which the Platform is materially unavailable for more than twenty-four (24) consecutive hours due to our fault.

2. Listing Quality Standards

2.1 Photo Requirements

All listing photographs must:

  • Be genuine, recent images of the actual property (no stock images, internet downloads, or digitally altered photos that misrepresent the property).
  • Clearly show the rooms, common areas, and amenities being advertised.
  • Not contain personally identifiable information of current tenants or third parties without their consent.
  • Not include watermarks from other platforms or promotional overlays.

2.2 Description Standards

Listing descriptions must:

  • Accurately state the accommodation type, room configurations, shared vs. private facilities, and included amenities.
  • Clearly disclose the gender designation (male-only, female-only, or co-living) and any house rules.
  • State the correct pricing, including whether bills (electricity, gas, water, internet) are included or separate.
  • Not contain misleading claims about proximity to institutions, transport, or facilities.
  • Not include contact information intended to take transactions off-platform.

Listings that fail to meet these standards may be unpublished or returned to the Host for revision. Repeated violations may result in account suspension.

3. Listing Verification Process

3.1 What "Verified" Means

When a listing carries the "Verified" badge, it means the listing has passed our internal review process, which includes:

  • Confirmation that the Host's identity has been verified through a valid CNIC or business registration document.
  • A review of the listing's photographs, description, and stated amenities for completeness and consistency.
  • Where feasible, a physical inspection or virtual walkthrough of the property.

3.2 What "Verified" Does Not Mean

The "Verified" badge is not:

  • A guarantee of the property's safety, structural integrity, or compliance with local building codes or fire safety regulations.
  • An endorsement or recommendation by HostelHive.
  • A certification of the Host's financial standing, legal status, or character.
  • A warranty that the property matches its listing description at all times — conditions may change after verification.

Seekers are strongly encouraged to conduct their own due diligence, including a personal visit to the property, before entering into any accommodation arrangement.

3.3 Ongoing Monitoring

We conduct periodic re-reviews of verified listings based on user reports, review patterns, and random audits. A listing may lose its "Verified" status if it fails a re-review or if we receive credible complaints about its accuracy.

4. User Support

4.1 Support Channels

Users may contact our support team through the following channels:

  • Email: support@hostelhive.com (available 24/7; responses within the timeframes below).
  • In-app help centre: accessible from the Account menu.

4.2 Response Times

We aim to respond to support inquiries within the following timeframes (during business hours, Monday–Saturday, 9:00 AM–6:00 PM PST):

  • Critical issues (account security, payment failures, safety concerns): within four (4) hours.
  • Standard inquiries (billing questions, listing issues, feature requests): within twenty-four (24) hours.
  • General feedback: within forty-eight (48) hours.

4.3 Escalation

If you are not satisfied with the initial response, you may request escalation by replying to the support thread with "Escalate." Escalated matters are reviewed by a senior team member within forty-eight (48) hours.

5. Content Moderation

5.1 Listing Content

All listing content (text, photographs, videos) must comply with the following standards:

  • Accurately represent the property and its current condition.
  • Not contain obscene, defamatory, discriminatory, or otherwise objectionable material as defined under PECA 2016 and the Pakistan Penal Code 1860.
  • Not infringe on any third party's intellectual property rights (Copyright Ordinance 1962).
  • Not include personally identifiable information of third parties (such as tenants) without their consent.

5.2 Review Content

User reviews are subject to moderation. We may remove or decline to publish reviews that:

  • Contain hate speech, personal attacks, or threats.
  • Are demonstrably false or defamatory (Pakistan Penal Code, Sections 499–502; PECA 2016, Section 20).
  • Appear to be fake, incentivised, or submitted by someone with no genuine experience of the listing.
  • Contain spam, promotional links, or irrelevant content.

5.3 Reporting and Takedown

Users may report content they believe violates this policy by using the in-app report function or emailing support@hostelhive.com. Reported content will be reviewed within forty-eight (48) hours. Content found in violation will be removed, and the offending user may face account suspension.

5.4 PTA and Government Takedown Orders

Under PECA 2016, Section 37, and the Removal and Blocking of Unlawful Online Content (Procedure, Oversight and Safeguards) Rules 2021, the Pakistan Telecommunication Authority (PTA) may direct us to remove or block access to content deemed unlawful. We will comply with valid PTA directions within twenty-four (24) hours of receipt, or within six (6) hours for emergency orders involving threats to national security, public order, or the glory of Islam. Content involving blasphemy (Pakistan Penal Code, Sections 295–298) will be removed immediately upon detection or credible report.

5.5 Consumer Protection Compliance

Our content moderation practices are designed to comply with consumer protection legislation across all Pakistani provinces, including the Punjab Consumer Protection Act 2005, Sindh Consumer Protection Act 2014, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Consumer Protection Act 1997, and Balochistan Consumer Protection Act 2003. If you believe a listing constitutes an unfair trade practice or contains misleading information, you may report it through the channels described above or file a complaint with the relevant provincial Consumer Court.

6. Subscription Plans and Billing

6.1 Plan Changes

Hosts may upgrade or downgrade their subscription plan at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately, with the prorated difference charged to the payment method on file. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.

6.2 Auto-Renewal

Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing cycle unless cancelled at least forty-eight (48) hours before the renewal date. Cancellation can be done from the Host dashboard under Subscription settings.

6.3 Price Changes

We may adjust subscription pricing with at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice. Existing subscribers will be offered the option to continue at their current rate until the end of their current billing cycle. Continued use after the price change constitutes acceptance of the new pricing.

6.4 Failed Payments

If a payment fails, we will attempt to process it up to three (3) times over a period of seven (7) days. If all attempts fail, the subscription may be suspended, and listed properties may be temporarily hidden until payment is resolved.

7. No Refunds

All payments to HostelHive are final and non-refundable, consistent with clause 6.3 of the Terms of Service. This applies to subscription fees, renewal charges, and one-time fees such as featured-listing promotions, and covers unused time remaining in a billing period, listings removed or suspended for breach, and account cancellation. Cancelling a subscription stops future renewals; it does not refund the current period.

This does not exclude any refund that cannot lawfully be excluded, including the reversal of a charge taken in error or duplicated. Report billing errors to support@hostelhive.com with your account email and the transaction reference.

8. Intellectual Property and DMCA-Equivalent Procedure

We respect the intellectual property rights of others and expect our users to do the same. If you believe content on the Platform infringes your copyright or trademark, you may submit a takedown notice to support@hostelhive.com with the following information:

  1. Identification of the copyrighted or trademarked work you claim has been infringed.
  2. Identification of the infringing material on the Platform with enough detail for us to locate it.
  3. Your contact information (name, address, email, phone number).
  4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that use of the material is not authorised by the rights holder.
  5. A statement, under penalty of perjury (Pakistan Penal Code, Section 193), that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the rights holder or authorised to act on their behalf.
  6. Your physical or electronic signature.

We will investigate valid notices and remove or disable access to infringing content within seventy-two (72) hours. Repeat infringers may have their accounts terminated.

9. Safety and Reporting

9.1 Safety Expectations

HostelHive is committed to fostering a safe environment. We expect all users to behave lawfully and with respect towards one another. Any conduct that threatens the physical safety, privacy, or dignity of another user is strictly prohibited.

9.2 Reporting Safety Concerns

If you experience or witness unsafe conditions at a listed property, harassment by another user, or any illegal activity, please:

  • Immediate danger: Contact local law enforcement (Police: 15, Rescue: 1122) before contacting us.
  • Report to HostelHive: Email support@hostelhive.com or use the in-app report function. Safety reports are treated as critical issues (four-hour response time).

9.3 Cooperation with Authorities

We will cooperate fully with law enforcement agencies and regulatory bodies in Pakistan, including the FIA and local police, in accordance with applicable law, including PECA 2016, Section 32 (provisions for real-time collection and recording of information).

10. Service Level for Hosts

10.1 Listing Publication

New listings submitted for verification will be reviewed and either approved, returned for revision, or rejected within five (5) business days of submission.

10.2 Payout Processing

Where HostelHive facilitates payments between Seekers and Hosts, payouts to Hosts will be processed within five (5) business days of the transaction clearing, subject to the Host's bank processing times.

10.3 Analytics and Reporting

Hosts on paid plans have access to listing analytics (views, inquiries, conversion rates) through the Host dashboard. Analytics data is updated on a daily basis and reflects activity from the previous calendar day.

11. Accessibility

We are committed to making the Platform accessible to all users, including those with disabilities. We strive to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards. If you encounter any accessibility barriers while using the Platform, please contact us at support@hostelhive.com with a description of the issue, and we will work to address it promptly.

We may update this Service Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, practices, or legal requirements. Material changes will be communicated via email or in-app notice at least fifteen (15) days before they take effect. Your continued use of the Platform after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
