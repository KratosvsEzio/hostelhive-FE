About Us

Making student accommodation safer and easier to find across Pakistan

Our Story

HostelHive was born out of a simple frustration: finding safe, verified student accommodation in Pakistan shouldn't be this hard. Every year, thousands of students move to new cities for education and struggle to find trustworthy housing — relying on word of mouth, unverified listings, and hoping for the best.

We started HostelHive to change that. Our platform connects students with verified hostel owners, giving seekers the transparency they deserve and giving hosts the tools they need to run their properties professionally.

What We Do

  • For students — search, compare, and book verified hostels with confidence. See real photos, honest pricing, and genuine reviews before you commit.
  • For hosts — manage your property end-to-end: rooms, tenants, invoicing, utilities, and mess. One dashboard, zero paperwork.

Our Mission

To become Pakistan's most trusted platform for student accommodation — where every listing is verified, every transaction is transparent, and every student can find a safe place to call home.

Why Trust HostelHive?

  • Verified listings — every hostel on our platform goes through a verification process before it goes live.
  • Transparent pricing — no hidden fees. What you see is what you pay.
  • Real reviews — only tenants who've actually stayed can leave a review.
  • Secure payments — all transactions are processed safely through our platform.
