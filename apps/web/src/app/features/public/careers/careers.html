Careers

Join us in building Pakistan's #1 hostel platform

HostelHive is on a mission to make student accommodation safer, more transparent, and easier to find across Pakistan. We're a small, fast-moving team and we're looking for people who want to build something that matters.

Why HostelHive?

  • Real impact — thousands of students rely on the platform every day to find safe, verified housing.
  • Small team, big ownership — every engineer, designer, and ops person shapes the product directly.
  • Move fast — we ship weekly and iterate based on real user feedback.
  • Remote-friendly — work from anywhere in Pakistan.

Open positions

We don't have any open positions right now, but we're always interested in hearing from talented people. If you think you'd be a great fit, reach out to us at support@hostelhive.com with your resume and a short note about why you want to join.

What we look for

  • Curiosity and a bias toward action — you'd rather ship and learn than debate endlessly.
  • Comfort with ambiguity — early-stage means the roadmap changes often.
  • Empathy for users — our students and hosts come first.
  • Clear communication — remote work demands it.
